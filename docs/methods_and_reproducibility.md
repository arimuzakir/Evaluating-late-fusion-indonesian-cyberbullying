# Methods and reproducibility

## 1. Cohorts, alignment exclusions, and five-role evaluation design (`R1.1`, `R1.4`, `R2.4`)

The frozen `v1.1-GoldAdjudicated` label inventory contains 13,307 observations (`CB_MASTER_00001..CB_MASTER_13307`): 8,906 standalone text comments (`CB_MASTER_00001..CB_MASTER_08906`) and 4,401 ten-second video windows (`CB_MASTER_08907..CB_MASTER_13307`, segmented with a 10 s window and 3 s stride from 233 source videos). The multimodal alignment filter retains **3,951** video windows across **214** linked source groups (`2,326` positive, `1,625` negative). The remaining **450** segmented windows (`280` positive, `170` negative; `62.22%` positive vs `58.87%` in the 3,951 retained windows) fail the duplicate/content-match alignment audit recorded in `results/statistics/revision_20261108/exclusions/EXCLUSION_REASON_PLATFORM_CLASS.csv` and `results/statistics/revision_20261108/exclusions/EXCLUSION_SOURCE_CHARACTERISTICS.csv`:
- `frame_identity_match` only: **197** windows
- `frame_identity_match+stt_content_match`: **200** windows
- `ocr_content_match` only: **45** windows
- `frame_identity_match+ocr_content_match`: **5** windows
- `frame_identity_match+ocr_content_match+stt_content_match` (all three failures): **3** windows

No model accuracy is imputed for these 450 excluded windows. The high-agreement standalone-comment benchmark retains **8,196** comments (`1,226` held-out test across `865` source groups). The two tracks are evaluated independently and are never pooled into a single accuracy metric.

In the primary video evaluation (`results/video/gold_adjudicated_v1.1/`), source groups are partitioned into four source-disjoint outer folds (`fold 0..3`) and five mutually disjoint roles within each fold (`fit`, `early_stop`, `meta_fit`, `calibration`, `test`), repeated across three training seeds (`42, 2026, 2027`). Per-fold role counts (`windows`, `groups`, `negative`, `positive`, and `text_windows`/`text_groups` where `ocr_ok == 1` or `stt_ok == 1`) are archived in `results/video/revision_20261108/roles/ROLE_COUNTS.csv`, and the role isolation rules for all **23 configurations** (21 primary models plus 2 seed-42 probability controls) are archived in `results/video/revision_20261108/roles/MODEL_ROLE_RULES.csv` and `configs/revision_20261108/ORIGINAL_ROLE_ALLOCATION_RULES.json`.

When both OCR and STT outputs are empty (`685` of `3,951` aligned windows: `423` positive, `262` negative), extracted-text branches receive a zero text feature vector and emit the neutral fallback probability `p = 0.5`. Because the positivity decision rule is **`probability >= threshold`** (`p >= τ*`), `p = 0.5` yields `all_positive` whenever the calibration-selected fold threshold satisfies `τ* <= 0.5` and `all_negative` whenever `τ* > 0.5` (`results/statistics/revision_20261108/fallback/TEXT_FALLBACK_DECISIONS.csv`). Exact per-fold and pooled fallback confusion matrices (`tn, fp, fn, tp`, `macro_f1`) across all 21 models × 3 seeds and the 2 seed-42 probability controls are archived in `results/statistics/revision_20261108/fallback/NO_TEXT_FALLBACK.csv` and `results/statistics/revision_20261108/fallback/CALIBRATION_CONTROL_FALLBACK.csv`.

Because the original protocol locks did not specify window- versus source-weighted priority for probability calibration, this hierarchy is explicitly documented as a revision-time reporting decision.

## 3. Reviewer-requested V4 sensitivity and control analyses

1. **Two additional 5-role main-pipeline source-group allocations (`R2.5`)**:
   To evaluate sensitivity to the source-group allocation on the fine-tuned main pipeline, two new four-fold, five-role (`fit`, `early_stop`, `meta_fit`, `calibration`, `test`) source-disjoint allocations were generated without inspecting prediction scores (`results/video/revision_20261108/source_partitions/PARTITION_SEED_LEDGER.csv`, `results/video/revision_20261108/source_partitions/PARTITION_1042_ROLES.csv`, `results/video/revision_20261108/source_partitions/PARTITION_2042_ROLES.csv`), and the focal **`text` vs `equal_vat`** branches (`indobenchmark/indobert-base-p2`, `EfficientNet-B0`, and `waveform16_v1` logistic regression) were trained from scratch at `training_seed = 42` (`results/video/revision_20261108/source_partitions/REPARTITION_EFFECTS.csv` and `results/video/revision_20261108/source_partitions/REPARTITION_PREDICTIONS.csv`; other auxiliary models and post-hoc controls were not retrained on these partitions):
   - **Partition `1042`** (`search_start_seed = 1042`, `effective_outer_seed = 1042`, `training_seed = 42`): `text_f1 = 0.5322441613114784`, `equal_vat_f1 = 0.547547707084874`, `delta = 0.015303545773395544`, 95% source-clustered bootstrap CI **`[-0.0013907197934134488, 0.032653003054556234]`**.
   - **Partition `2042`** (`search_start_seed = 2042`, `effective_outer_seed = 2044`—the first candidate seed from `2042` satisfying fold window/class/platform constraints—with `training_seed = 42`): `text_f1 = 0.5469555003724585`, `equal_vat_f1 = 0.541611762016599`, `delta = -0.005343738355859551`, 95% source-clustered bootstrap CI **`[-0.02085725995059201, 0.01140696636205858]`**.
   - **Bootstrap group-ordering rule and provenance (`R2.5`)**: In `REPARTITION_PREDICTIONS.csv`, video windows are indexed by `master_id` (`CB_MASTER_08907..CB_MASTER_13307`) and pseudonymized source groups `src_<16hex>`. In the archived revision notebook (`src/reference_archived/model_and_fusion_reference.py`), `paired_ci()` was executed before `save()` replaced `group_id` with `src_<16hex>` pseudonyms. In the public repository, ordering the 214 unique `group_id` values by their first appearance after sorting rows by `master_id` (`pd.unique(d.sort_values('master_id').group_id)`) in `scripts/analysis/reproduce_revision.py` is the **public deterministic ordering rule** that reproduces `REPARTITION_EFFECTS.csv` (`text_f1`, `equal_vat_f1`, `delta`, `ci_low`, `ci_high`) to **`0.0` (`atol = 1e-12`)**; no unrecorded claim is made regarding how the withheld pre-pseudonymization source identifiers were originally ordered. If the 214 groups are instead ordered lexicographically by their `src_<16hex>` pseudonym strings (`np.unique(d.group_id)`), the multinomial bootstrap draw indices shift the 95% CI quantiles by at most `4.42e-4` (`[-0.0015131384766076195, 0.032329495427405756]` for `1042`; `[-0.02062388609164806, 0.010964716587871032]` for `2042`), while point estimates are identical. Both rules are verified at `atol = 1e-12` in `scripts/validation/verify_revision.py`.
