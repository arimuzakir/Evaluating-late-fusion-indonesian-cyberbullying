# Interpretation and validity

- **Operational construct**: The target label is content-level *bullying-indicative aggression* within a standalone comment or a ten-second video window. A positive window is not independent proof of repetition, power imbalance, perpetrator intent, or victim age.
- **Cross-track non-comparability**: The standalone-comment benchmark (`n = 8,196`, held-out test `n = 1,226`) and the aligned-video task (`n = 3,951` windows across `214` source groups) use distinct populations and filtering protocols. Their Macro F1 values are not comparable as a modality ablation.
- **Exploratory history and retrospective inference (`R1.1`, `R2.4`)**: Source-group disjointness prevents identifiable window leakage across folds, but historical exploratory development included examination of earlier held-out runs (`docs/provenance/revision_20261108/PROVENANCE_TIMELINE.csv`). Retrospective focal comparisons and the retrospective minimum detectable effect (`SE = 0.0075125`, `MDE = 0.0210469` at 80% power, two-sided `α = 0.05`) must not be interpreted as prospective preregistration.
- **Main-pipeline source-partition sensitivity vs frozen-feature probes (`R2.5`)**: The primary three training seeds (`42, 2026, 2027`) share the baseline 4-fold source partition. In the V4 revision, two additional full main-pipeline source-group allocations were completed at training seed `42`: partition `1042` (`effective_outer_seed = 1042`, `Δ(Equal VAT − Text) = +0.01530`, 95% CI `[−0.00297, +0.03356]`) and partition `2042` (`search_start_seed = 2042`, `effective_outer_seed = 2044`, `Δ(Equal VAT − Text) = −0.00534`, 95% CI `[−0.02353, +0.01264]`). Across all three outer source allocations (`42`, `1042`, `2042`), the confidence intervals span zero. The five additional partitions in `REPEATED_SPLIT_SUMMARY.csv` and the cross-platform LOPO evaluation remain frozen-feature linear probes.
- **Seed-42 probability controls and estimand selection timing (`R1.4`, `R2.4`)**: The two revision probability controls (`sigmoid_equal_vat` and `convex_vat`) are available for **seed 42 only**. Window-weighted metrics are reported as the primary estimand and equal-source-weighted metrics as a cluster-size sensitivity (`TABLE3_RECOMPUTED.csv`, `TABLE6_PROBABILITY_METRICS.csv`); selecting window-weighted as primary was a revision-time reporting decision rather than a preregistered protocol.
- **Neural-head sensitivity and runtime reconciliation (`R1.2`)**: The 48-run 2×3×2 factorial sensitivity grid at seed 42 (`results/video/revision_20261108/head_sensitivity/`) was executed in a separate session where `torch.set_float32_matmul_precision('high')` was not recorded, producing small differences (`−0.0126` to `+0.0040` Macro F1) relative to frozen Table 3 in the matched hyperparameter cell (`HEAD_BASELINE_RECONCILIATION.csv`). Frozen Table 3 is retained unchanged for primary reporting.
- **Audit finite-population bounds and unidentified failure mechanism (`R1.5`, `R2.6`)**: Keyframe `46/9/0` is the reproducible item-level score distribution among the 55 positive audited windows (`44/8/3` historical coordinator aggregate documented separately). Conservative 95% Bonferroni-hypergeometric bounds (`OCR: [0.23342, 0.99459]`; `STT: [0.24302, 0.99042]`) bound extraction fidelity within the finite 3,951-window cohort under worst-case unaudited completions and **must not** be interpreted as validation on new external sources. Subgroup error-transition confidence intervals in `AUDIT_ERROR_TRANSITIONS.csv` remain `NA` due to small audited subgroup sizes (`n = 9 to 51`), and the specific causal mechanism of fusion failure remains unidentified.
- **Alignment exclusions (`R1.6`)**: The 450 excluded video windows (`359` missing video files, `91` missing keyframe images; `62.22%` positive vs `58.87%` in the 3,951 retained windows) are profiled in `EXCLUSION_REASON_PLATFORM_CLASS.csv` and `EXCLUSION_SOURCE_CHARACTERISTICS.csv`. Platform post-stratification reweights retained windows only and cannot identify performance on excluded windows.
- **Three-level reproduction boundary**: Raw media, unredacted comments/OCR/STT text, and fine-tuned neural checkpoints are withheld. Public scripts reproduce all metrics, statistical intervals, finite-population bounds, and frozen-feature probes from archived numerical outputs, while `src/reference_archived/` provides reference training code only.
- **Administrative record vs institutional ethics determination (`R1.1`)**: The signed Universitas Bina Darma record (`010/DRPM/Univ-BD/IX/2026`, dated 23 September 2026; see [`data_release.md`](data_release.md) and [`UBD_Data_Provenance_and_Protection_Record_Signed_20260923.pdf`](UBD_Data_Provenance_and_Protection_Record_Signed_20260923.pdf)) is an administrative data provenance and protection record. It is **not** an institutional ethics approval, an ethics-exemption determination, or a platform redistribution license; formal institutional ethics determination remains an open item (`OPEN`).
