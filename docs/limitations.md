# Interpretation and validity

- The operational target is content-level bullying-indicative aggression. A positive ten-second window is not independent evidence of repetition, power imbalance, intent or victim age.
- The standalone-comment benchmark and aligned-video task use different input and filtering protocols. Their F1 values are not comparable as a modality ablation.
- Source-group disjointness reduces identifiable leakage among windows. Previous exploratory examination of test results means the retrospective evaluation is not an untouched prospectively registered test.
- Three seed runs share the same outer source partitions. Additional five-partition variation applies only to separate frozen-feature linear probes, not the principal fine-tuned pipeline.
- Cross-platform LOPO probes are on frozen representations and do not establish end-to-end transportability of the main models.
- Keyframe 46/9/0 is a reproducible row-level score distribution for the 55 positive audited windows, not a corpus-wide coverage estimate. A different historical coordinator aggregate remains archived without item-level support; no new human adjudication is claimed.
- Retained and excluded video observations differ in positive-class proportion; poststratification does not estimate results on excluded windows.
- The archived waveform16 representation cannot support reconstruction of the exact original source waveform-to-feature map from public data.
- Raw media, original comments and full checkpoints are restricted. Published scripts support recomputation from stored probability outputs and selected frozen features.
- Administrative data provenance, usage, de-identification, and release controls are documented in the signed institutional record (`010/DRPM/Univ-BD/IX/2026`, dated 23 September 2026; see [`data_release.md`](data_release.md) and [`UBD_Data_Provenance_and_Protection_Record_Signed_20260923.pdf`](UBD_Data_Provenance_and_Protection_Record_Signed_20260923.pdf)). As stated in that record, administrative acknowledgment is not an institutional ethics approval, an ethics-exemption determination, or a blanket platform redistribution license.
- The effect of fusion on Macro F1 is not supported as positive or equivalent by the recorded clustered confidence interval.
