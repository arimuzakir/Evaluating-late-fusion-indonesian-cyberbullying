# Interpretation and validity

- **Operational construct (`R2.1`)**: The target label is content-level *bullying-indicative aggression* within a standalone comment or a ten-second video window. A positive window is not independent proof of repetition, power imbalance, perpetrator intent, or victim age. As recorded in `docs/provenance/revision_20261108/CLAIM_SCOPE_DECISIONS.json`, the study evaluates a bounded comparison of specified fixed and learned late-fusion implementations rather than modern multimodal cyberbullying detection in general.
- **Cross-track non-comparability**: The standalone-comment benchmark (`n = 8,196`, held-out test `n = 1,226` across `865` source groups) and the aligned-video task (`n = 3,951` windows across `214` source groups) use distinct populations and filtering protocols. Their Macro F1 values are not comparable as a modality ablation.
- **Exploratory history and retrospective inference (`R1.7`, `R2.2`)**: Source-group disjointness prevents identifiable window leakage across folds, but historical exploratory development included inspection of earlier held-out runs (`docs/provenance/revision_20261108/RESEARCH_CHRONOLOGY.csv`). While `configs/analysis/control_protocol_lock.json` (`2026-10-26T12:06:40Z`) locked the two post-hoc seed-42 probability controls before their execution, it does not establish prospective preregistration for the full study history or the final four-contrast families (`results/statistics/FOCAL_FOUR_100000.csv` and `docs/provenance/revision_20261108/COMMENT_MULTIPLICITY_FAMILY.csv`). Likewise, the unadjusted retrospective normal-approximation minimum detectable effect (`bootstrap_se = 0.0075124837897468`, `mde_unadjusted = 0.021046863536667928` at 80% power, two-sided `α = 0.05` in `docs/provenance/revision_20261108/ROUNDING_AND_MDE.json`) is a conditional precision diagnostic, not a prospective power calculation or equivalence proof.
- **Main-pipeline source-partition sensitivity vs frozen-feature probes (`R2.5`)**: The primary three training seeds (`42, 2026, 2027`) share the baseline 4-fold source partition. In the V4 revision, two additional 5-role source-group allocations were completed at `training_seed = 42` for the focal **`text` vs `equal_vat`** contrast (`results/video/revision_20261108/source_partitions/REPARTITION_EFFECTS.csv` and `results/video/revision_20261108/source_partitions/PARTITION_SEED_LEDGER.csv`): partition `1042` (`effective_outer_seed = 1042`, `text_f1 = 0.5322441613114784`, `equal_vat_f1 = 0.547547707084874`, `delta = 0.015303545773395544`, 95% CI `[-0.0013907197934134488, 0.032653003054556234]`) and partition `2042` (`search_start_seed = 2042`, `effective_outer_seed = 2044`, `text_f1 = 0.5469555003724585`, `equal_vat_f1 = 0.541611762016599`, `delta = -0.005343738355859551`, 95% CI `[-0.02085725995059201, 0.01140696636205858]`). Across the baseline and both additional allocations, the 95% confidence intervals span zero; these internal repartitions reuse the same 214 source groups and are not external validation on new sources. The five partitions in `results/generalization/REPEATED_SPLIT_SUMMARY.csv` and the cross-platform LOPO evaluation remain frozen-feature linear probes.
- **Seed-42 probability controls and estimand selection timing (`R1.4`, `R2.6`)**: The two revision probability controls (`sigmoid_equal_vat` and `convex_vat`, both fitted on `meta_fit` with thresholds selected on `calibration`) are available for **seed 42 only** (`results/statistics/revision_20261108/probability/TABLE6_PROBABILITY_METRICS.csv`, `results/statistics/revision_20261108/probability/CALIBRATION_CONTROL_BRIER_INTERVALS.csv`). Both reduce window Brier loss relative to `text` (`window_brier = 0.2491781368632344` [rank 2] and `0.2492549371758121` [rank 3] vs `text` `0.282976145655324` [rank 5] and `vat_lr` `0.25391349224340776` [rank 4]), and achieve lower equal-source-weighted Brier (`source_brier = 0.214898353943736` [rank 1] for `sigmoid_equal_vat` and `0.218641645807787` [rank 3] for `convex_vat` vs `equal_vat` `0.2206605012012136` [rank 4]), but remain worse in primary window Brier and thresholded Macro F1 (`0.5019314267553432` and `0.5209103528213086`) than uncalibrated `equal_vat` (`window_brier = 0.2361663196632117` [rank 1], `window_macro_f1 = 0.5669772833605011`). Designating window-weighted metrics as the primary estimand and equal-source-weighted metrics as a cluster-size sensitivity (`configs/revision_20261108/PROBABILITY_ESTIMAND.json`) was a revision-time reporting decision rather than a preregistered rule.
- **Neural-head sensitivity and runtime reconciliation (`R1.3`)**: The 48-fit 2×3×2 factorial sensitivity grid at seed 42 (`concat_mlp` and `healnet` / `healnet_adapted_2024` across epochs `{15, 30, 60}` and weight decay `{0.01, 0.1}` across 4 folds; `results/video/revision_20261108/head_sensitivity/HEAD_SENSITIVITY_POOLED.csv`) yields pooled Macro F1 of `0.4876617530360183` (`concat_mlp_e60_wd0.1`) to `0.5054619174213288` (`concat_mlp_e30_wd0.1`) for `concat_mlp`, and `0.48995107844566277` (`healnet_e60_wd0.01`) to `0.5094701632707828` (`healnet_e15_wd0.01`) for `healnet`. Both the original Gold run and the sensitivity run recorded an **NVIDIA L4** GPU and identical fold scaler bytes, but differed in `numpy` (`2.1.3` vs `2.4.6`), `torch` (`2.11.0+cu130` vs `2.14.1+cu130`), `torchvision` (`0.26.0+cu130` vs `0.29.1+cu130`), Linux kernel (`Linux-6.6.122+-x86_64-with-glibc2.39` vs `Linux-6.6.159+-x86_64-with-glibc2.39`), and `float32_matmul_precision` settings (`docs/provenance/revision_20261108/TRAINING_ENVIRONMENT_COMPARISON.csv`, `results/video/revision_20261108/head_sensitivity/HEAD_BASELINE_RECONCILIATION.csv`); causal attribution of the baseline replay discrepancy (`-0.006237174777324217` for `concat_mlp`, `-0.01594976382203317` for `healnet_adapted_2024`) remains unresolved. Frozen Table 3 is retained unchanged for primary reporting.
- **Audit finite-population bounds and unresolved failure mechanism (`R1.5`, `R2.3`)**: Keyframe `46/9/0` (`2`: `captures focal event`, `1`: `partial event or context`, `0`: `focal event in clip not captured by frame` in `results/input_audit/revision_20261108/AUDIT_RATING_DEFINITIONS.json`) is the reproducible item-level score distribution from independent raters `R1` and `R2` (`Form_Audit_R1_scored.csv`, `Form_Audit_R2_scored.csv`) and `Form_Adjudikasi.csv` among the 55 consensus-positive audited windows (`44/8/3` historical coordinator aggregate documented separately). Conservative 95% Bonferroni-hypergeometric bounds across the 20 `availability × platform` strata (`OCR: [0.2334152334152334, 0.9945945945945946]` over `2,035` available windows; `STT: [0.2430167597765363, 0.9904229848363927]` over `2,506` available windows) bound extraction fidelity within the fixed finite 3,951-window cohort and **must not** be interpreted as validation on new external sources. Subgroup error-transition confidence intervals in `results/input_audit/revision_20261108/AUDIT_ERROR_TRANSITIONS.csv` remain **`NA`** due to sparse discordant source groups (`0 to 7`), and the specific causal mechanism of fusion failure (as well as temporal pooling effects archived in `results/video/revision_20261108/temporal_archive/EXISTING_TEMPORAL_RESULTS.csv`) remains unresolved.
- **Alignment exclusions and historical extraction logs (`R1.2`, `R2.4`)**: The 450 excluded video windows (`197` `frame_identity_match`, `200` `frame_identity_match+stt_content_match`, `45` `ocr_content_match`, `5` `frame_identity_match+ocr_content_match`, and `3` `frame_identity_match+ocr_content_match+stt_content_match` in `results/statistics/revision_20261108/exclusions/EXCLUSION_REASON_PLATFORM_CLASS.csv`) are profiled in `results/statistics/revision_20261108/exclusions/EXCLUSION_SOURCE_CHARACTERISTICS.csv`. Platform post-stratification reweights retained windows only and cannot identify performance on excluded windows. In addition, historical extraction code (`docs/provenance/revision_20261108/EXTRACTION_EVIDENCE.csv`) converted runtime OCR/STT exceptions to empty strings without retaining per-window exception logs or exact historical package/checkpoint hashes.
- **Three-level reproduction boundary (`R1.6`)**: Raw media, unredacted comments/OCR/STT text, and fine-tuned neural checkpoints are withheld. Public scripts reproduce all metrics, statistical intervals, finite-population bounds, and frozen-feature probes from archived numerical outputs, while `src/reference_archived/` provides reference training code only, and `results/figures/Fig_1.png` and `results/figures/Fig_2.png` are manual author diagram exports whose editable Draw.io sources are maintained outside the public repository.
- **Administrative record vs institutional ethics determination (`R2.4`)**: The signed Universitas Bina Darma record (`010/DRPM/Univ-BD/IX/2026`, dated 23 September 2026; see [`data_release.md`](data_release.md) and [`UBD_Data_Provenance_and_Protection_Record_Signed_20260923.pdf`](UBD_Data_Provenance_and_Protection_Record_Signed_20260923.pdf)) is an administrative data provenance and protection record. It is **not** an institutional ethics approval, an ethics-exemption determination, or a platform redistribution license; formal institutional ethics determination remains an open item (`OPEN`).
